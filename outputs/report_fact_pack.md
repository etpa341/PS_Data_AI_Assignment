## B. Definitions

- Themes (8): Staff attitude; Communication and updates; Wait time and turnaround; Price and billing; Work quality and fix; Vehicle or product; Process and facilities; Other [config/settings.yaml]. Sentiment: positive, neutral, negative, mixed [config/settings.yaml].
- Score: (x - min) / (max - min) * 100 on each file's true scale; Geelong 2025 reversed (x -> 6 - x) first [profiling/data_governance.xlsx, Decisions_Log D1].
- Dissatisfied = score below 40: 1-2 of 5 (also 1-2 stars and the two lowest text labels), 1-4 of 10, 'Bad' at Tamworth. At risk = score 50 or below: 3 or less of 5, 5 or less of 10, 'OK' or 'Bad' at Tamworth [Decisions_Log D1].
- Voice tag: regex heuristic marking staff third-person write-ups ('Cust said...') as staff_paraphrased; themes use customer voice only [dq_rule_log.csv R13: 206 rows tagged].
- no_comment: included row with no comment text; tagged without an AI call [03_label.py docstring].
- Responses with a score: 7,060 [q1_by_department.csv]; with comment text: 5,349 [derived_numbers.csv]; customer voice with text (AI-labelled): 5,150; no_comment 1,721 (24.3%); staff write-ups skipped 199 [sanity/label_status.csv].

## G. Pipeline and tech choices

- Layers: bronze = every row as read (gitignored: unredacted); silver = cleaned, redacted, excluded rows flagged; gold = silver + AI labels + revenue panel [README.md, data_dictionary.md].
- Scripts: profiling/profile.py (governance workbook); 01_bronze.py (read 52 files, reconcile); 02_silver.py (clean, redact, validate, rule log); select_model.py (newest Haiku for the key); build_prompt.py (prompt from Definitions tab); 03_label.py (cached AI labels; offline by default); 04_evaluate.py (AI vs hand labels); 05_gold.py (gold + panel); analysis.ipynb (Q1-Q3); derived_numbers.py; make_labelling_sheet.py, make_extra_check.py, evaluate_extra_check.py, make_spot_check.py, make_report_pdf.py, make_fact_pack.py, evaluate_definitions.py, make_figures.py, make_deliverables.py [README.md].
- Language and libraries: Python; pandas, openpyxl, pyyaml, matplotlib, pytest; anthropic for live labelling only [README.md].
- Cache: data/silver_labels/cache.jsonl keyed by sha256(text + prompt version + model) with raw responses; offline rerun gives identical labels [03_label.py docstring; ai_usage_log.md].
- Fresh-clone test: offline, no key, 0 API calls; silver, gold and all 42 outputs byte-identical [ai_usage_log.md].
